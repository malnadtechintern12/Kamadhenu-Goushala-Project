Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Non-Profit Trust Nature

Kamadhenu Goushala is operated by the Kamadhenu Goushala Seva Trust, a registered non-profit charitable trust in Karnataka, India. All donations and proceeds from organic products are utilized solely for animal welfare, veterinary care, shelter maintenance, and indigenous breed conservation.

2. Voluntary Donations & Tax Exemption

All financial contributions made to the trust are voluntary. Donations are non-refundable once an official 80G tax receipt has been generated. In the rare event of an accidental duplicate transaction, donors may contact our trust office within 7 days with transaction references.

3. Sanctuary Visits & Code of Conduct

Visitors to Kamadhenu Goushala sanctuary are requested to maintain a respectful, peaceful, and clean demeanor. Feeding cows must only be done with approved green fodder or feed provided by the sanctuary staff to protect cattle health.

4. E-Store & Shipping Policy

Orders placed for organic manures and puja products are dispatched within 2-3 business days. While we take utmost care with packaging, damaged products upon arrival will be replaced upon photo verification.
