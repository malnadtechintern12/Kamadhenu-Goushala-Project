Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Kamadhenu Goushala Seva Trust collects personal information provided voluntarily by donors, sponsors, visitors, and customers, including names, contact numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, and PAN numbers (strictly for issuing 80G tax exemption receipts).

2. Use of Information

Your personal data is used solely to:

  • Issue official 80G tax exemption donation receipts and sponsorship certificates.
  • Process store orders and coordinate delivery of organic farm items.
  • Send monthly cow health updates and sanctuary festival newsletters.
  • Respond to visit inquiries and volunteering registrations.

3. Payment Security & Data Protection

All financial transactions through our donation portal and e-store are encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption. We do NOT store credit card details or net banking credentials on our servers. All payments are processed through RBI-authorized payment gateways.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade donor information to any third parties. Data is only shared with Indian regulatory tax authorities when generating mandatory 80G income tax returns.

5. Contacting the Trust

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to update your email preferences, please write to us at info@kamadhenugoushala.org.
