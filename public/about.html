About Kamadhenu Goushala

Rooted in Vedic tradition and ethical animal compassion, serving 500+ indigenous Indian cows with lifelong care.

Our Sacred Heritage

Nurturing Desi Cows as Divine Mothers

In Indian tradition, Gau Mata is celebrated as the embodiment of universal maternal affection, harboring 33 crore divine deities within her serene presence. Indigenous Indian cows (Bos Indicus) are not merely livestock; they are the bedrock of organic soil vitality, spiritual auspiciousness, and human well-being.

Founded in 2015 by a dedicated assembly of Vedic scholars, veterinary specialists, and organic farmers, Kamadhenu Goushala was established to rescue abandoned, distressed, and sick cattle, granting them unconditional love and sanctuary without commercial exploitation.

"Gavo Vishwasya Matarah"

The Cow is verily the Mother of the entire Universe. (Rig Veda)

Sanctuary Foundations

Our Guiding Pillars

Lifelong Ahimsa Protection

No cow or bull in our care is ever sold, culled, or abandoned when milk production ceases. Every animal lives peacefully until natural old age.

Ayurvedic & Veterinary Hospital

Combining modern diagnostic imaging, blood tests, and fracture stabilization with traditional herbal decoctions, turmeric oils, and Panchagavya healing.

Eco-Friendly Solar Campus

100% solar powered water pumps, organic bio-gas generation, rain-water harvesting, and circular manure recycling for zero ecological footprint.
